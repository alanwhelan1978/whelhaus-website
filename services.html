02

SEO

Being online isn't enough.
You need to be found.

SEO is not about stuffing pages with keywords or making unrealistic promises about being number one on Google.

It is about making your website technically sound, easy to understand, relevant to the people searching, and structured so search engines can properly evaluate it.

SEO support can include

  • SEO website audits
  • Keyword and search intent research

  • Page titles and meta descriptions

  • Google Business Profile optimisation
  • Technical SEO improvements
  • Content recommendations
