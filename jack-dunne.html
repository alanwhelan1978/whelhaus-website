Jack Dunne is an established Gorey menswear business with a strong reputation for quality products, personal service and a loyal customer base.

The brief was to create a modern, mobile-friendly website that reflected the in-store experience. The work focused on clear information, straightforward navigation and a search-friendly foundation for people looking for menswear in Gorey.

The challenge

A strong business needed a stronger digital presence.

The existing online presence did not fully reflect the quality, personality or professionalism of the retail business.

The project needed to create a clearer brand story, improve usability and give customers a stronger first impression online.

The response

Simplify. Sharpen. Make it useful.

The site was approached as a commercial tool rather than a digital brochure.

The website was organised around the essentials: introducing the business, presenting its menswear offering and helping visitors find a route to contact or visit the shop. Responsive layouts, page structure and on-page SEO fundamentals supported that approach.
