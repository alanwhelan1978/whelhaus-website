The project

Jack Dunne is an established menswear business with a strong reputation for quality products, personal service and a loyal customer base.

The goal was to create a digital presence that felt as considered as the in-store experience, while making the business easier to discover, understand and engage with online.

The challenge

A strong business needed a stronger digital presence.

The existing online presence did not fully reflect the quality, personality or professionalism of the retail business.

The project needed to create a clearer brand story, improve usability and give customers a stronger first impression online.

The response

Simplify. Sharpen. Make it useful.

The site was approached as a commercial tool rather than a digital brochure.

Clear navigation, stronger messaging, cleaner design and search-friendly structure were used to create a more confident and effective customer journey.
