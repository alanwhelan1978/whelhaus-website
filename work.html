Project 01 · Gorey

Jack Dunne Menswear

Web Design · Local SEO · Digital Strategy

Jack Dunne is an independent menswear retailer that has served Gorey since 1993. The business needed a website that felt as established and considered as the service customers receive in store.

The challenge

The previous online presence did not explain the full offer or reflect the quality of the business. Customers needed a clearer route to menswear, wedding and formal hire, gift vouchers and practical store information.

What WHELHAUS delivered

A seven page responsive website with focused service pages, stronger messaging, straightforward navigation and clear contact routes. The build also includes on page SEO, structured data and local search foundations.

The outcome

The completed site now gives Jack Dunne a professional digital home that matches the character of the shop and makes its main services easier to understand on mobile and desktop.

Project 02 · Ballygarrett

Room & Groom

Web Design · Local SEO · Digital Strategy

Room & Groom is a family run dog boarding, daycare and grooming business in Ballygarrett, County Wexford. It needed one clear, credible place to present its services and turn interest into enquiries.

The challenge

Boarding, daycare and grooming are different customer needs. The website had to explain each service clearly, establish trust quickly and make it easy for dog owners to call or send an enquiry from a phone.

What WHELHAUS delivered

A responsive multi page website with dedicated service information, prominent enquiry routes and a design system tailored to the business. The site also includes local search signals and a clear information structure.

The outcome

Room & Groom now has a polished website that explains the offer without clutter. It gives referrals and search visitors a useful next step, whether they need boarding, daycare or grooming.
