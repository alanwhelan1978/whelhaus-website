JACK DUNNE

Web Design · SEO · Digital Strategy

A website design project for Jack Dunne, an established menswear retailer in Gorey, Co. Wexford. The brief was to bring the quality and personal service of the shop into a clearer, more useful online presence.

WHELHAUS worked on responsive web design, navigation, messaging and technical SEO foundations, with clear routes for customers to learn about the business and get in touch.
